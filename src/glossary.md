# Glossary

**ALOHA.** A Linear Open-Hardware Accelerator: our family of tabletop electron machines.

**ALOHA-0.** Our electron source: a thermionic source in telefocus configuration, designed for 20 keV, with its vacuum, high-voltage, control and diagnostic systems.

**ALOHA-1.** An electron linear accelerator of up to 1 MeV, in design. It is built only once a university laboratory can host it.

**ALOHA-2.** The X-ray fluorescence instrument for paintings that we aim to build around ALOHA-0.

**Model-0.** Our glow-discharge demonstrator, used for outreach.

**UPV Accelerator Challenge.** Our yearly event for UPV students: workshops by the team and presentations by the participants.

**Direction.** The Tutor, the Coordinator and the Co-Lead.

**Tutor.** The member of UPV teaching staff who holds final authority on safety and on institutional matters, and who appoints the Coordinator and the Co-Lead.

**Coordinator.** The student responsible for the team as a whole.

**Co-Lead.** The student who shares the coordination with the Coordinator and deputises for them.

**Department.** One of the four groups in which members learn and qualify: Applied Physics, Electronics and Control, Technical Design, and Communication.

**Department lead.** The member, selected by Direction, who mentors a department's candidates, assigns its members to tasks and reviews its work.

**Shadower.** The member a department lead chooses in the second semester to take over the role the following season.

**Task team.** A group formed around one deliverable and dissolved when the deliverable is accepted.

**Convener.** The member who handles the administration of a task team.

**Standing responsibility.** A function held by a named member through the year, such as safety or procurement.

**Candidate.** A participant in the trial period.

**Member.** A participant admitted by Direction at the end of the trial.

**Qualified.** A Member with demonstrated competence in one area, allowed to work independently in it.

**Contributor, collaborator.** A person or institution that takes part without joining the team.

**Design Decision Record (DDR).** A short document stating a decision, the alternatives considered and the reason.

**Pull request.** A proposed change to a repository, reviewed before it is merged.

**INNOVA-REP, INNOVA-TN.** Our numbered series of reports and papers, and of technical notes.

**Laboratory logbook.** The internal record of every operation of laboratory equipment.

**Logbook.** The series on our website in which we write up our work for the public.

**Cold test.** A low-power radiofrequency measurement of an accelerating cavity, made without beam.

**Telefocus.** An electron source whose electrode geometry places the focus far beyond the anode.

**X-ray fluorescence (XRF).** A technique that identifies the elements in a material from the X-rays they emit when excited.

**Generación Espontánea (GE).** The UPV's programme for student teams.
