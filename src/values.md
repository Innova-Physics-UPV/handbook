# Values

We expect the following of every participant, in all technical work.

- **Measurement decides.** A simulation is a hypothesis until a measurement agrees with it.
- **Every number carries its unit and its status.** Measured, simulated, design or estimated, with its tolerance, its provenance and, where relevant, its convention: full or half angle, peak or average.
- **Aims are stated as aims.** We say what we aim to do, and we claim only what we have done.
- **Negative results are reported.** A design that failed, a geometry that would not converge or a supplier that did not deliver is written down promptly.
- **Work is documented.** Members turn over every two or three years. The standard is that a competent stranger can continue the work from the repository alone.
- **Safety rules are followed exactly.** Nobody operates hazardous equipment without training and authorisation.
- **Nothing is understood by only one person.** A subsystem that a single member can explain is a defect to be corrected, and every role is handed over with overlap.
